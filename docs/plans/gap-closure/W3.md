## G27 and G28: probes

`cl_probe.py`: V = 1 V, R = 1 k, node a, C(stim) to ground, stim steps 0 -> 1 at 5 us (edge 1 ns, 10 ns, 1 us),
C = 1 nF + 1 nF*stim. Reference: dq/dt = (V - q/C(t))/R integrated with scipy LSODA (rtol 1e-12); q is continuous across
the step, v jumps to C0/C1. `ll_probe.py` is the dual (flux, current).

| form | max error vs charge/flux reference | vs voltage/current reference |
|---|---|---|
| `C='C0+CS*V(stim)'` / `L='..'` | 0.50 V (v stays at 1 V: charge lost) | 0 |
| `flux='L*i(L1)'` | 0.50 V (same as `L=`) | 3e-15 |
| `B ... I=ddt(C*V(a))` (the old B-DDT form) | 1e307 (diverges) | 1e307 |
| **`C1 a b q='C(stim)*V(a,b)'`** | **3e-6 V at tstep 1 ns, 8e-5 V at 10 ns** | 0.50 V |
| Eq/Cu/Vz/Fq charge construction | identical to `q=` | |
| **flux construction** (Ephi = L*I(Vz), unit C, Hv) | **2e-6 V at 1 ns, 4e-5 V at 10 ns** | 0.37 to 0.50 V |

Errors are the same for edges of 1 ns, 10 ns and 1 us, and there is no spike at the step: the artifact of B-DDT is gone
because ngspice's own capacitor integrates the smooth charge. Package forms:
- HUMIDITY_CAP: `VSTIM stim 0 DC {RH}`, `C1 a b q='(C0+CS*V(stim))*V(a,b)'`. Native, one line.
- SOLENOID: `VSTIM`, `R1 a n1`, `Vz n1 x 0`, `Ephi ph 0 VALUE={(LO+(LC-LO)*V(stim))/LO*I(Vz)}`, `Cu ph y {LO}`, `Vzs y 0 0`,
  `Hv x b Vzs 1`. The `/LO` and `Cu = LO` keep V(ph) in amps, so the solver tolerances act on a sensible scale.

Tests (`test_value_checks_w3.py`, live `replay_document`, ideal `alter` steps): humidity 140 -> 260 pF at 1 V: v jumps to
C0/C1 and relaxes with R*C1 to 3 mV at k = 0.01, 0.5, 1, 2, 4 tau; 260 -> 140 pF likewise plus the charge
integral (C1 - C0) to 3 %; plunger 20 -> 60 mH: i falls from 1 A to 1/3 A and relaxes with 6 ms to 4 mA; 60 -> 20 mH
rises to 3 A and falls with 2 ms. The old forms would stay at 1 V / 1 A and fail these by 0.4 V / 0.67 A.
Existing static tests (RC tau, L/R rise at both ends) pass unchanged. `rh` and `pos` are live stimulus params
(`test_live.py` STIMULI).

Limits: no initial voltage or current can be set on the two parts (the charge/flux state comes from the operating point).

## G29: interval evaluation (`benchcore/src/interval.rs`, wired in `validate.rs`)

What is evaluated: B sources, E/G `VALUE=`, and behavioural R/C/L (`r= c= l= q= flux=`), per instance, over
- every package number that declares both `min` and `max` (its `[min, max]`, exclusive ends open), passed by the catalog as a
  `.param bp_<key>` plus `benchcore.validate(deck, ranges)` (new optional argument, `validate::validate_with`);
- every other param at its default;
- `V(node)` of a node a `V` source holds against ground (DC value as an expression, or the hull of SIN, PULSE, EXP, PWL);
- a `.dc`-swept source or param is dropped to unknown.
Everything else (`V(x)` of a circuit node, `I(vsrc)`, `@dev[..]`, `temper`, user `.func`, `ddt`, `pwl`, `%`) is unknown.

Probed ngspice 47 behaviour (`domain_probe.py`) that sets the rules: sqrt/ln/log/log10 of a negative are fatal; `ln(0)` is
silently -1e99; `1/0` is silently 1e32; `exp` above 227.9 is silently clamped to 1e99; `pow` of a negative base with a
non-integer exponent and `pow(0,-1)` are silently 0; `asin(2)` etc. give NaN; a product or power past f64 is fatal;
`2^3^2` is 64 (left associative) and `-2^2` is -4. The parser follows that.

Findings: B-DOMAIN, B-DIVZERO, B-OVERFLOW (errors, value class), each naming the operation and a witness
(`(at LUX = 0)`); B-CL-FORM (warn). An *error* is only raised when exact point arithmetic reproduces it (samples: ends,
quarters, middle, 0; a divisor positive at one sample and negative at another counts as a zero). A hit no sample
reproduces, and every operation on an unknown range, stays LIMITED-ANALYSIS with the operation named (`division by
V(m): its range is not known`, `cannot rule out: ...`). `pow(0, <0)` at an open-ended 0 (`lux` in (0, 1e5]) is a pole, not a hit.
A second pass evaluates with every node and branch current at 0 (ngspice's first iterate) and reports only fatal
functions (`sqrt(V(in)-4)`).

### Which sensors moved to `full` (and why that is honest)
All nine B-driven sensors, plus MOV and SAT_AMP (batch A): LDR, PTC_THERMISTOR, RTD_PT100, RTD_PT1000, FSR, STRAIN_GAUGE,
STRAIN_BRIDGE, POSITION_SENSOR, PRESSURE_BRIDGE. The hazards in their expressions are the ones the check covers: a divisor
that is a function of params and the stimulus (LDR `R10*pow(lux/10,-g)`, FSR, RTD `1+A*T+B*T^2` over 0..850 degC, strain
`1+GF*eps*1e-6` over +-1e4 ue, PTC `exp(-(T-TC)/W)` over -55..200), pow bases, exp arguments. POSITION_SENSOR and
PRESSURE_BRIDGE have no hazard at all (a product), MOV `pow(abs(v)/VC, a)` has a non-negative base and SAT_AMP is
`max/min`. The tests show the check bites: widening the declared range past the law (lux closed at 0, eps +-5e5, PT100 to
8000 degC, PTC to -1000 degC) makes the catalog reject the part with a witness.
Parts that stay limited: the 30 with XSPICE `A` devices or the `T` line (not B-related).

### Stated limits (what `full` does not claim)
- Only a number with both bounds is a range; a one-sided param (R10 > 0) is checked at its default. The user's own value is
  checked when the deck runs (a point), so `rleak = 0` is refused then.
- Live edits beyond the declared range are not covered (the slider ranges are the declared ones).
- A node of unknown range is trusted not to overflow a power or product; only domain and zero hazards are asked of it.
- Interval arithmetic cannot see that two uses of one name are equal; such a case is `limited`, never an error.
- Dependency between a derived param and its root is lost in sampling (contrived).
- The DC rule of a B `I=` source (a conductance when it reads its own port voltage) is unchanged and still heuristic.
